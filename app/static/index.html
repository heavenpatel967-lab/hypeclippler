HypeClipper

Watches live chat, clips the hype, writes captions and titles. Free and local.
Windows' page file is too small for HypeClipper. This is what causes "WinError 8 / not enough memory". Fix it once: double-click fix_memory.bat in the HypeClipper folder, click Yes, then restart your PC.

Downloads & uploads

⬇ Downloads
Auto download saves every finished clip (9:16 mp4 + a .txt with title, caption and hashtags). With "Delete after download" it then disappears from this dashboard. Turn both on in Quick switches.
▶ YouTube
One-time setup with a free Google Cloud project, see README "Uploading to YouTube".
📷 Instagram
Needs a Professional (Creator/Business) account and a free Meta developer app, see README "Uploading to Instagram".

Branding (thumbnail, title, description)

🖼 Thumbnail / cover
The same image on every YouTube Short and Instagram Reel. Wide images are fitted on a blurred 9:16 background.
📝 Title & description template
Placeholders: {title} {headline} {description} {streamer} {streamer_mention} {platform} {stream_link} {game} {extra_mentions} {hashtags}. {streamer_mention} is the streamer's @handle from "@ tags" on their card.

    

  


  
Sound effects

  
Each folder is a type of sound. The app picks sounds that fit the moment (fails get FAHHH, emotional damage, sad violin; shocks get vine boom, taco bell, metal pipe; laughs get laugh sounds...). Add sounds by uploading a file or pasting a link: a direct .mp3 link or a sound page like myinstants.com. Your sounds replace the built-in one in that folder.
